## Why do these recipes not have measurements?
When I cook something, I rarely follow an exact recipe. I typically start with some inspiration from the internet – Adam Ragusea, Jacques Pepin, and Internet Shaquille are some of my favorites.

Once I actually start cooking, I play things by ear, modifying the ingredients, quantities, and techniques as I go. By experimenting and refining a dish over time, I develop a general framework for how to cook it. Eventually, I have made the same thing enough times that I can gauge quantities by eye.

Thus, none of the recipes here are step-by-step guides with exact quantities. Rather, they are frameworks that serve as reminders for me and (hopefully) inspiration for others.
