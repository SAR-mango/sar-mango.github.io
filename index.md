## Welcome
Hi, I'm Erk! I'm an electrical engineer designing satellite avionics at SpaceX.

I graduated from UC Santa Barbara with my electrical engineering degree in June 2026. I plan to pursue a PhD in the future – see my research interests below.

This website is where I share my thoughts about engineering and various other topics. I also created some reference pages that some may find helpful.

## About Me
Electrical engineering has been a lifelong passion of mine. I like projects involving a mix of power electronics and embedded systems, especially when applied to RC drones and planes (see [SinESC](https://github.com/SAR-mango/SinESC)).

I'm mostly into hardware, but I also enjoy programming for a direct hardware application. I'm also obsessed with writing stuff in [LaTeX](https://github.com/SAR-mango/latex-templates).

I'm from San Diego and have lived in California all my life.
