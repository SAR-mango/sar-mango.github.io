## Welcome
Hi, I'm Erk! I'm an electrical engineer at SpaceX, designing avionics for satellites.

I graduated from UC Santa Barbara with my electrical engineering degree in June 2026. I plan to pursue a PhD in the future – see my research interests below.

This website is where I share my thoughts about engineering and various other topics.

## About Me
Electrical engineering has been a lifelong passion of mine. I do projects involving a mix of power electronics and embedded systems, often applied to RC drones and planes (see [SinESC](https://github.com/SAR-mango/SinESC)).

I'm mostly into hardware, but I don't shy away from software development. I enjoy embedded C programming, paired with the typical CS nerd tools (Linux, `vim`, `git`, etc). I'm also obsessed with writing stuff in [LaTeX](https://github.com/SAR-mango/latex-templates).

I'm from San Diego and have lived in southern California all my life.
