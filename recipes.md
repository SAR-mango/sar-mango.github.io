When I cook something, I rarely follow exact recipes. I typically start with some inspiration from the internet – Adam Ragusea, Jacques Pepin, and Internet Shaquille are some of my favorites. Once I actually start cooking, I play things by ear, modifying the ingredients, quantities, and techniques as I go. By experimenting and refining a dish over time, I develope a general framework for how to cook the thing. Eventually, I have made the same thing enough times that I can gauge quantities by eye. Thus, none of the recipes here are step-by-step guides with exact quantities. Rather, they are frameworks that serve as reminders for me and (hopefully) inspiration for others.
